HOW TO PLAY

Hold and move to grow

Press and hold ahead of the vine. Move your mouse or finger to steer. You don't need to grab the plant.

First move

Start at the pot. Hold over the orange flower on the lower right.

✿

Get the flowers

Reach all three flowers to clear a garden. Growing uses water; blue drops refill it.

⌁

Catch bugs

Grow around a bug, then join back onto your vine to close the loop. Catches give water back. Keep loops small.

↯

Use Burst

Press Shift while growing, or tap Burst then grow. It protects you from bugs, not ledges.

Ⅱ

Pause and rewind

Letting go stops the vine, but bugs keep moving. Pause freezes everything. Rewind backs up the vine but costs score.

Keyboard controls
Arrows + G
Aim; hold G to grow.
Shift
Burst.
Space / Esc
Pause. Esc resumes.
Z
Rewind.
R
Retry.
Paused while you read.
