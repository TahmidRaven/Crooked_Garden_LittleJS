HOW TO PLAY

YOU CONTROL THE SUNLIGHT

The vine follows your light.

Hold anywhere ahead of the growing tip. Your mouse or finger becomes its sunlight. Move while holding to bend the vine—you do not drag the seed itself.

01

Try this first

In the first garden, find the glowing sprout in the pot at the bottom. Hold over the orange bud on the lower right. Once it blooms, guide the tip up the right side of the lowest mossy ledge toward the blue water drop.

⌁

Loop a bug. Catch it.

Grow around a bug, then touch older stem to close the loop. A catch refunds 75% of that loop's growth water (up to 240), plus 70 water and 300 points per bug. Each extra bug in the same catch adds another 80 water and 200 points. Tight loops nearly pay for themselves; oversized loops still cost water. Plan catches before the tank is empty. Each bug only pays once, even after rewind.

✿

Bloom all three

Touch every closed flower bud with the growing tip. The three flowers in the HUD light up as you save them. Restore all three to unlock the next garden; finish all three gardens to complete a run.

◆

Keep your vine watered

Growing spends water. Blue drops restore 260 water. Take useful detours, but avoid wasting growth on loops. Running out of water ends the attempt.

↯

Burst through danger

Press Shift while growing, or tap Burst to arm it, then grow. It gives a short speed boost and bug shield for 30 water. It does not protect against solid mossy ledges. Blooming a flower stuns the bugs and refreshes Burst. Stunned bugs keep their colours but lose their glow and show stars.

!

Read the swarm

Wanderers roam, hunters turn toward you, and chargers show a warning line before lunging. Watch for a gap or use Burst. Releasing stops the vine, not the bugs. Use Pause to freeze everything safely.

Controls & second chances

Mouse / touch
Hold and move to grow toward your light.
Arrows + G
Aim the light; hold G to grow.
Shift
Protected Burst while growing.
Space / Esc
Pause. Use Resume or Esc to continue.
Z / Rewind
Back up a mistake, with a short recovery shield. Costs 100 score.
R / Retry
Restart this garden with a new swarm.
The game stays paused while you read.
