HOW TO PLAY

01

Touch, drag and grow

Touch or click anywhere in the garden. A translucent joystick appears there. Drag in the direction you want to grow. Release or centre it to stop.

Tap Burst with your other thumb while steering.

02

Flowers are the goal

Reach all three to clear a garden. Growing uses water, so pick up blue drops along the way.

03

Make a loop to catch bugs

Grow around a bug, then join back onto your vine. Catches give water back. Keep loops small.

04

Burst past danger

Press Shift while growing, or tap Burst then grow. It protects you from bugs, not ledges. Stars mean a bug is stunned.

Ⅱ Pause↶ Rewind

Letting go stops the vine, but bugs keep moving. Pause freezes everything. Rewind backs up the vine but costs score.
