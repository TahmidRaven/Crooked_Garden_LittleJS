HOW TO PLAY

01

Drag to grow

Touch or click anywhere in the garden. Drag to steer; release to stop.

02

Wake every flower

Reach all three. Collect blue drops to keep your water flowing.

03

Loop to catch bugs

Circle a bug and reconnect to your vine. Small loops earn water back.

04

Burst past bugs

Tap Burst or press Shift, then grow. Safe from bugs, not ledges.

Bugs keep moving when you let go. Pause to take a breath.

Rewinds & retries

Per run: 3 rewinds (−100 pts each) and 3 retries (−300 pts each). They do not refill between gardens.

Keyboard controls
